## ✨ What can you do with Brevolka?

⛽ **Fuel log and fill-up tracker**
Log date, odometer, quantity, price and total cost. See mileage and fuel efficiency (km/l, L/100 km or MPG) after every fill-up.

💰 **Car and bike expense tracker**
Track service, repairs, insurance, parking, tolls and more. Know your true cost per km or mile.

🚗 🏍️ 🛵 🚚 **Multiple vehicles**
Add cars, motorbikes, scooters and trucks. Keep each vehicle's history separate.

📊 **Charts and summaries**
See monthly and yearly fuel spend, efficiency and total expenses at a glance.

🌍 **Your units, your currency**
Kilometres or miles, litres or gallons, any currency.

💾 **Export and backup**
Export your records and keep a backup file you control.
